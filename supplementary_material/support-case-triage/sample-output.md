# Support Case Report

## Case Reference

`ISSUE-NNN`

## Reported Problem

Describe the customer's or support agent's reported experience.

## Customer Impact

State the observable impact in plain language.

## Evidence Reviewed

| Question | Evidence | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investigation question | Customer report, log, request, or source artifact | Fact established by the evidence |

## Assessment

- **Confidence:** Confirmed, likely, possible, or insufficient evidence.
- **Current assessment:** State the evidence-based conclusion, or the specific unanswered question.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** State what evidence is still needed.

## Next Actions

| Owner | Action | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | Specific action | High, normal, or low |

## Customer Communication

Write a brief factual response. Do not include technical causes, timing promises, compensation offers, or internal details.
