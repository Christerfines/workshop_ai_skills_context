Leaderboard Scoring (for context, facilitator-run)

FinalScore = (Q × 70) + (M × 30) − Penalty, where Q = share of cases scoring ≥16/20 and passing all 6 gate items, M = mean cost-efficiency vs. the Tier-3/19.8k-token baseline, and Penalty = 10 points per critical adversarial-archetype failure. Correctness (Q) dominates the score — cost savings alone cannot compensate for a wrong or ungrounded determination.
