# Vinter Pro

## Overview

The Vinter Pro is NordicBike AB's winter e-bike — a purpose-built model for riders who commute and ride through Swedish winters rather than parking their bike from November to March. It pairs a rear hub motor with studded tires as standard equipment, a drivetrain and cable routing designed to tolerate road salt and slush, and a battery management approach tuned for cold-weather performance and longevity. The Vinter Pro is the second most common bike in this workshop's support case load, and it is the model most likely to be brought in for warranty service outside the city riding season.

Development of the Vinter Pro started from the observation that most e-bikes on the Swedish market, including NordicBike's own Aurora X3, are optimized primarily for three-season use, with winter riding treated as an edge case rather than a core scenario. The Vinter Pro inverts that assumption: everything from tire choice to cable routing to display placement assumes the bike will regularly encounter snow, slush, road salt, and sub-zero temperatures, and is built and warrantied with that as the expected, normal use case rather than an exception.

The Vinter Pro was launched with a dedicated marketing push timed to build early-season demand ahead of its first full winter on the market, positioning it specifically for riders in northern and central Sweden — regions where months of the year involve genuinely challenging winter cycling conditions rather than the milder, shorter winters typical of the far south. Customer feedback since launch has centered mainly on the studded tires and rear hub motor's reliability in the conditions the bike was designed for, with cold-weather range being the most common practical question raised by prospective and existing owners alike.

The rear hub motor was chosen over a mid-drive for the Vinter Pro specifically for its simpler, more weatherproof drivetrain interface — fewer exposed moving parts near the chain and cassette reduce the chance of salt and slush intrusion during winter riding, and a rear hub motor keeps the drivetrain's more failure-prone components away from road spray thrown up by the front wheel. Studded tires come as standard equipment rather than an optional upgrade, reflecting the bike's intended year-round, all-weather use case; the studs improve traction on icy and packed-snow surfaces at some cost to rolling resistance and on-road noise compared to a standard tire, a tradeoff intentionally made in favor of winter safety.

Cable routing on the Vinter Pro runs internally through the frame where practical, reducing the exposed cable length that would otherwise be most vulnerable to salt corrosion over a winter season. The display unit uses a larger, higher-contrast screen than would be strictly necessary in milder conditions, chosen specifically for readability in low winter light and with gloved-hand operation in mind. Brake lines and calipers are specified with cold-weather-rated seals, since standard hydraulic fluid and seal materials can behave differently at the sustained sub-zero temperatures the Vinter Pro is expected to operate in.

## Available Configurations

The Vinter Pro is available in one color and three frame sizes:

- **Sizes:** S, M, L
- **Colors:** Arctic White

The single-color approach for the Vinter Pro was a deliberate choice distinct from the Aurora X3's two-color lineup — Arctic White was selected partly for visibility in low-light winter riding conditions, since a light-colored bike reflects more of the limited daylight and any available street lighting during the shortest days of the year, which NordicBike considered a genuine, if modest, visibility benefit for winter commuters riding to and from work in the dark.

## Battery & Range

The Vinter Pro uses the same integrated PowerPack 720 battery (720Wh) as the Aurora X3. Under typical mild-weather conditions, range is up to 65 km on a full charge. Cold weather has a measurable effect on lithium battery performance, and the Vinter Pro's range is reduced by approximately 20% in temperatures below 0°C — riders should plan for roughly 52 km of effective range on especially cold days, and less under demanding conditions such as deep snow or strong headwinds. NordicBike recommends storing the battery indoors, at above-freezing temperatures, between rides during the coldest part of the winter where practical, since extended exposure to deep cold when not in use can further reduce a battery's effective range and, over time, its longevity — though this is a usage recommendation rather than a warranty condition, and does not itself create or remove any warranty coverage.

The Vinter Pro's battery management firmware is tuned specifically for cold-weather charging, moderating charge rate at low ambient temperatures to protect cell health — this is normal, expected behavior and can mean a full charge occasionally takes modestly longer than the standard 4.5-hour figure on especially cold days, which is not itself indicative of any fault.

The LED light set is a particularly common add-on purchase for Vinter Pro owners specifically, given the reduced daylight hours the bike is most often ridden in during peak winter months, though it remains an optional accessory rather than standard equipment and carries the same accessory-level warranty terms described in products/accessories.md regardless of which bike it's paired with.
