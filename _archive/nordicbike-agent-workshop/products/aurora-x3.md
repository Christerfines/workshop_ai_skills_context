# Aurora X3

## Overview

The Aurora X3 is NordicBike AB's flagship city e-bike and the company's best-selling model. It is designed as an all-purpose daily commuter for riders who want a confident, low-maintenance electric bike for year-round use in urban and suburban conditions — office commutes, grocery runs, school drop-offs, and general city riding. The Aurora X3 pairs a mid-drive motor with an integrated battery, a lightweight aluminum frame, and a component set chosen for durability over years of daily use rather than for maximum performance on any single metric.

The Aurora X3 sits at the center of NordicBike's product line: it is the bike most new customers buy first, the bike most frequently brought in for routine warranty service, and the bike referenced most often in this knowledge base. Two customer-facing archetypes in this workshop — the Symptom-Cause Confusion pair and the Out-of-Scope Escalation case involving an unauthorized motor modification — are both built around the Aurora X3, because its combination of an integrated battery and an electrically assisted drivetrain is exactly where symptom-versus-root-cause distinctions and modification-related exclusions become practically important.

Since its introduction, the Aurora X3 has gone through incremental component refinements — updated brake calipers, a revised display bracket, a firmware-updatable controller — without changing its core specification numbers below, which remain the reference figures for every unit currently in service and every unit sold going forward. Any support conversation involving an Aurora X3 should treat the specifications in this document as current regardless of the exact manufacturing date of the unit in question, except where a specific service bulletin (see Section 5 of policies/warranty.md) calls out a batch-specific issue.

The Aurora X3 is positioned deliberately as a generalist rather than a specialist: it is not the cargo-hauling workhorse the Fjord Cargo is, and it is not purpose-built for deep winter riding the way the Vinter Pro is, but it covers the largest share of everyday use cases well — flat and moderately hilly city terrain, typical Nordic weather short of deep winter conditions, and the kind of daily mileage a commuter accumulates over a normal working week. Riders who later find they need more cargo capacity or dedicated winter equipment are, in NordicBike's experience, more often supplementing an Aurora X3 with a second, more specialized bike than replacing it outright — which is part of why the Aurora X3 remains in service, and in this support case load, for years after purchase.

Because it is the highest-volume model in NordicBike's lineup, the Aurora X3 is also the model most likely to appear at the edges of every archetype covered elsewhere in this knowledge base, not only the two it is explicitly the vehicle for. A support agent should expect a disproportionate share of routine, non-adversarial cases — ordinary wear-item replacements, straightforward manufacturing-defect claims well inside the standard window — to involve an Aurora X3 simply because there are more of them in the field than any other model. That volume is exactly why the symptom-versus-root-cause distinction embodied in Service Bulletin SB-2025-11 matters so much in practice: a batch-level manufacturing issue on the company's best-selling model touches far more customers, and far more visually similar-but-differently-caused claims, than the same kind of issue would on a lower-volume model.

## Typical Use Cases

The Aurora X3 is most commonly purchased for:

- **Daily commuting** — the single largest use case by purchase volume, typically a round trip in the 5–25 km range, well within the eco-mode range figure even accounting for return-trip and minor detours.
- **Errands and short local trips** — grocery runs, picking up or dropping off children, trips to a train or bus station as part of a longer multimodal commute.
- **Recreational city and suburban riding** — weekend rides on paved paths and low-traffic roads, where the 8-speed derailleur's range is generally sufficient for the gentle grades found in and around most Swedish cities.

The Aurora X3 is not marketed or warrantied for off-road use, competitive racing, or sustained loads beyond what a single rider and a moderately packed pannier bag would represent; use substantially outside these patterns is more likely to raise questions about the accident/misuse exclusion in policies/warranty.md Section 4(iv) than about a manufacturing defect, though the two are not the same thing and should not be assumed interchangeable without more specific information about what actually happened.

## Maintenance & Servicing

Routine maintenance for the Aurora X3 — brake pad inspection, drivetrain lubrication, tire pressure checks, and general safety inspection — is available at any of NordicBike's three partner service centers (Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö) regardless of where the bike was originally purchased. Routine maintenance visits are separate from warranty claims: a scheduled service visit to replace worn brake pads at the customer's own cost due to ordinary mileage is not a warranty matter, whereas a brake pad that fails prematurely due to a genuine manufacturing defect within the Section 2 wear-item window is. NordicBike recommends a basic safety and drivetrain check roughly once per year for a typically-used commuter bike, more frequently for higher-mileage riders, though this is a general usage recommendation rather than a warranty condition — failing to have the bike serviced on any particular schedule does not, on its own, void any part of the Aurora X3's warranty coverage.

| Motor | 250W mid-drive |
| Battery | PowerPack 720, integrated, 720Wh |

| Serial format | AX3-[YY][A\|B]-[5 digits], e.g. AX3-25A-00417 (YY = year of manufacture, A = Jan–Jun, B = Jul–Dec) |

The mid-drive motor placement was chosen over a hub motor for better weight balance and more natural pedaling feel, particularly when climbing or carrying a loaded pannier bag. Mounting the motor low and centered, near the bottom bracket, keeps the Aurora X3's center of gravity close to where a rider would expect it on a conventional bicycle, which matters for low-speed handling in city traffic — tight turns, stop-and-go riding, and maneuvering in bike parking. The 8-speed derailleur gives enough range for both flat city streets and the occasional hill without adding the maintenance overhead of an internal gear hub, and it uses widely available, easily serviced components rather than a proprietary drivetrain, which keeps routine servicing quick at any of NordicBike's three service centers.

The hydraulic disc brakes are sized for confident stopping power in wet conditions, which is a meaningful consideration for a bike marketed as an everyday, all-weather commuter rather than a fair-weather-only bike. Brake pads on the Aurora X3 are a wear item under policies/warranty.md Section 2, with a 6-month coverage window for manufacturing defects specifically — ordinary pad wear from regular commuting mileage is expected and is not itself a defect. The aluminum 6061 frame is chosen for its balance of weight, stiffness, and corrosion resistance — an important property given that many Aurora X3 owners store their bikes outdoors or in unheated spaces such as bike sheds, garages, or shared building bike rooms rather than climate-controlled interior storage.

The LCD display shows speed, remaining battery percentage, current assist level, and trip distance, and pairs over Bluetooth with NordicBike's companion smartphone app for ride history, firmware updates, and basic diagnostics. The companion app is optional — the bike is fully functional without ever connecting it to a phone — and connectivity issues with the app are a software/support matter distinct from the bike's own hardware warranty coverage.

## Available Configurations

The Aurora X3 is available in three frame sizes and two colors:

- **Sizes:** S, M, L
- **Colors:** Nordic Black, Fjord Blue

All frame sizes and colors share identical drivetrain, motor, and battery specifications — sizing affects only frame geometry and standover height, and color is a cosmetic choice with no effect on warranty terms or component specification. Frame size is generally selected based on rider height, with size S suited to shorter riders, size M suited to average height riders, and size L suited to taller riders; NordicBike's online store provides a sizing guide at the point of purchase, and a customer's frame size selection at purchase is recorded as part of the order but does not affect any of the specification figures in this document.

Nordic Black and Fjord Blue are both a matte, low-glare powder-coat finish chosen for scratch resistance in daily commuter use rather than a high-gloss finish, since a commuter bike parked outdoors and locked to street furniture accumulates far more everyday wear than a bike ridden only occasionally. Neither color affects the frame material, weight, or any structural specification — the choice is purely cosmetic, and a customer switching between the two in a support conversation about a different topic entirely (for example, confirming which bike they own before a warranty claim) is not a signal of anything beyond personal preference at time of purchase.

## Battery & Range

The Aurora X3 uses an integrated PowerPack 720 battery (720Wh), the same battery model used as the integrated pack in the Vinter Pro and sold separately as a standalone spare part. Under eco mode and typical rider weight and terrain, the Aurora X3 achieves up to 80 km of range on a full charge; actual range varies with rider weight, terrain, assist level, tire pressure, and ambient temperature, with cold-weather use reducing effective range somewhat (see products/vinter-pro.md for the explicit cold-weather range figure that applies to that model's battery and tire configuration).

A full charge from empty takes 4.5 hours using the standard 4A charger included with every Aurora X3. The battery is charged in place on the bike; it does not need to be removed for charging, though it can be removed for storage or for battery-only warranty inspection. The charging port and battery compartment are sealed against normal rain and wet-road spray under intended use, but are not designed to withstand direct high-pressure spray from a pressure washer aimed at or near the compartment — see policies/warranty.md Section 4(i) for the exclusion this creates, and policies/warranty.md Section 5 for how this interacts with Service Bulletin SB-2025-11 specifically.

Riders who notice reduced range or charging irregularities are encouraged to first rule out simple causes — cold weather, low tire pressure, a heavier-than-usual cargo load, or a higher assist mode than usual — before assuming a battery fault, though genuine battery or connector faults do occur and are evaluated under the applicable warranty terms when they do. A sudden change in charging behavior (for example, a bike that previously charged fully and reliably now failing to hold a charge overnight, or losing power intermittently mid-ride) is a more meaningful signal of a possible fault than a gradual, modest reduction in maximum range over a long ownership period, which is more consistent with ordinary battery aging.

## Compatible Accessories

The Aurora X3 is compatible with all four items in NordicBike's accessories catalog (see products/accessories.md for full details and pricing):

- Pannier bag
- LED light set
- Frame lock
- Phone mount

Mounting points for all four accessories are standard equipment on every Aurora X3 frame regardless of size or color — a customer does not need any special edition or configuration to add any of these accessories after purchase. None of these accessories, once installed, constitute a modification to the drivetrain or electrical system for the purposes of policies/warranty.md Section 4(ii); they are explicitly supported, catalog accessories rather than aftermarket modifications, and installing any or all of them does not affect the bike's warranty status.

This distinction — catalog accessory versus aftermarket modification — is worth stating plainly because both can be described by a customer in similar terms ("I added something to my bike"). A pannier bag, light set, frame lock, or phone mount from products/accessories.md is a supported add-on with no warranty consequence. An aftermarket motor controller, a non-NordicBike battery, or a third-party performance kit affecting the drivetrain or electrical system is a different category entirely, and falls under the Section 4(ii) exclusion regardless of how minor the customer believes the change to be. When a support case mentions an addition to the bike, checking whether it matches an item in products/accessories.md is a fast way to rule out a Section 4(ii) concern before assuming one applies.

## Frequently Confused Questions

A few points come up often enough in support conversations to note explicitly here, even though each is also addressed in more detail in its own governing document:

- **"Is the PowerPack 720 the same battery whether it's integrated or bought separately?"** Yes, physically — but the warranty term differs. An Aurora X3's integrated battery is covered under the bike's own 24-month Section 1 term; a PowerPack 720 bought standalone as a spare or replacement is covered under the shorter 12-month Section 3 term instead. See products/powerpack-batteries.md and policies/warranty.md Section 3.
- **"Does riding in the rain void the warranty?"** No — normal rain and wet-road riding is expected, intended use. Only water damage from sources like high-pressure washing directed at or near the battery compartment falls under the Section 4(i) exclusion.
- **"Does the color or frame size affect the price or warranty?"** No — all configurations are priced identically at 34,900 SEK and carry identical warranty terms; size and color are cosmetic and ergonomic choices only.
