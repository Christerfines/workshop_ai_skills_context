**Professor Iris Vance:** Notice what we're not doing there: not one number, not one category, not one rule. Just — four documents exist, here's the kind of question each one answers. **Mira Chen:** What about the case-specific material?

**Marcus Webb:** And here's the navigation habit worth having before you're doing this live, under time pressure: for any given case, you'll typically need to cross-reference the case file, the matching customer record, the matching product file, and some subset of the four policy documents.
