# NordicBike AB — Shipping & Repair Logistics

This document covers the logistics of getting a bike or battery to a service center for warranty inspection or repair, and the turnaround time customers should be told to expect. It does not restate warranty eligibility terms (see policies/warranty.md) or escalation/authority rules (see policies/escalation.md) — it is purely operational, and none of the figures in this document should be used to determine whether a claim is eligible; they describe only what happens logistically once eligibility has already been established elsewhere.

## Service Center Locations

NordicBike AB operates through three partner service centers, each able to receive bikes and batteries for warranty inspection, repair, and claims processing:

- Stockholm (co-located with company headquarters, Hammarby Sjöstad)
- Gothenburg
- Malmö

A customer may use whichever service center is most convenient for them; there is no requirement to use the service center nearest to the original point of purchase, and no NordicBike policy penalizes a customer for using a different one. A customer in northern Sweden, for instance, is free to ship to whichever of the three centers has the shortest transit time from their location, which may not be the center geographically nearest to where they happen to live if road or courier routing makes a different center faster in practice.

## Repair Turnaround

Once a bike or battery has been received and logged at a service center, standard **repair turnaround time is 5–10 business days from intake**, covering diagnosis, any necessary parts ordering, the repair or replacement itself, and quality check before return to the customer. Turnaround can vary somewhat depending on parts availability and service center workload, but 5–10 business days is the figure to communicate to customers as the expected range. Customers should be told this range rather than a single fixed number, since actual turnaround genuinely does vary within it depending on the specific repair and current service center load.

This turnaround figure is generic and applies across warranty repair types; it is not restated with different numbers per case or per product in this knowledge base. Where a support response needs a repair-timing figure, this is the figure to cite, regardless of which product or which warranty section (Section 1, 2, 3, 5, or 6) the underlying claim was resolved under.

## Shipping Costs for Warranty Claims

- **Warranty-eligible claims:** shipping to the nearest service center is **free (NordicBike-paid)**. NordicBike provides a prepaid shipping label or arranges collection, depending on the item and the customer's location.
- **Non-warranty repairs** (i.e., repairs the customer is paying for out of pocket because the issue is not covered under warranty, or coverage has lapsed): the **customer pays shipping both ways** — to the service center and back.

Determining which of these two shipping-cost rules applies depends entirely on the underlying warranty eligibility determination made under policies/warranty.md; this document does not itself decide eligibility, only the shipping-cost consequence of whichever determination has already been made. In practice, this means the shipping-cost answer to give a customer should always come after, not before, the eligibility determination is settled — a support agent should not quote a shipping cost to a customer before knowing whether their claim is eligible, since quoting the wrong one and then having to correct it creates unnecessary confusion.

## Practical Notes for Support Conversations

A few points are worth keeping in mind when communicating shipping logistics to a customer alongside a warranty determination:

- **Packaging for shipment.** Customers shipping a bike for warranty service should be advised to package it reasonably securely — the original shipping carton if still available, or an equivalent bike box — though NordicBike does not require a specific packaging standard as a condition of warranty coverage, and reasonable, good-faith packaging is sufficient.
- **Standalone battery shipment.** Batteries, including PowerPack 720 and PowerPack 900 units sent in for standalone warranty inspection under policies/warranty.md Section 3, are shipped separately from a bike and are subject to standard courier regulations for lithium-ion battery transport; NordicBike's shipping labels and instructions account for this automatically when a battery-only claim is opened, so a customer does not need to research shipping regulations themselves.
- **Multiple service centers en route.** A customer does not need to ship to, or collect from, the same service center — for example, a bike could in principle be dropped off in Gothenburg and collected in Stockholm if that better suits the customer's travel plans, though NordicBike's default process assumes pickup and drop-off at the same location unless the customer requests otherwise.
- **Escalated cases.** Where a case has been escalated per policies/escalation.md, shipping logistics for any separable, non-escalated warranty repair proceed independently and are not held up pending the escalation's resolution, consistent with the general principle that an escalation on one part of a case should not block progress on an unrelated, already-resolved part of the same case.
