# PowerPack Batteries

## Overview

PowerPack batteries are NordicBike AB's standalone spare and replacement battery line. The same physical battery packs used as integrated batteries in NordicBike's bikes are also sold separately, so a customer can buy a spare for longer trips, replace an aging pack without replacing the whole bike, or restore a bike to full range after capacity has degraded over years of use. There are two PowerPack models, differentiated by capacity and by which bike they integrate into. Standalone PowerPack purchases carry their own warranty term, independent of and shorter than the whole-bike warranty term — this is the key fact this document exists to make unambiguous, since it is easy to mistakenly assume every NordicBike component carries the same 24-month term as a whole bike.

Both PowerPack models use the same lithium-ion cell chemistry and the same battery management system architecture, differing mainly in cell count and pack capacity to match the power demands of the bikes they're designed for. Over the ordinary lifespan of a NordicBike e-bike, some gradual reduction in maximum usable capacity is expected as the battery goes through repeated charge cycles — this is a normal characteristic of lithium-ion batteries generally, not specific to NordicBike's packs, and gradual, expected capacity fade over years of normal use is different from a sudden or unusually rapid capacity loss, which may indicate a genuine defect rather than ordinary aging.

Standalone PowerPack sales serve several distinct customer needs: a rider wanting a fully charged spare for a longer trip without waiting for a mid-ride recharge; an owner of an older bike whose original battery has degraded past a usable point and who wants to restore full range without replacing the whole bike; and, occasionally, a customer purchasing a PowerPack independently of any NordicBike bike purchase at all, for use with a compatible setup obtained elsewhere. NordicBike's records do not always distinguish between these motivations, and doing so is not necessary to determine a standalone battery's warranty status — only the standalone purchase date matters for that determination, under policies/warranty.md Section 3.

## PowerPack 720

- **Capacity:** 720Wh
- **Price (standalone):** 6,900 SEK
- **Compatibility:** Aurora X3 and Vinter Pro — as the original integrated battery or as a replacement part
- **Charging:** Standard 4A charger, full charge in 4.5 hours

The PowerPack 720 is the battery integrated into both the Aurora X3 and the Vinter Pro. A standalone PowerPack 720 purchased as a spare or replacement is functionally identical to the battery that ships integrated with either of those two bikes, and can be swapped in as a direct replacement without any other modification. Because it is used across two different bike models, the PowerPack 720 is NordicBike's higher-volume battery product, and is the more commonly seen battery in standalone-purchase support cases. A PowerPack 720 purchased standalone works identically whether it ends up in an Aurora X3 or a Vinter Pro — there is no model-specific variant of the pack itself, only a difference in how each bike's firmware manages range and cold-weather behavior around the shared battery.

## PowerPack 900

- **Capacity:** 900Wh
- **Price (standalone):** 8,900 SEK
- **Compatibility:** Fjord Cargo
- **Charging:** Standard 4A charger, full charge in 5.5 hours

The PowerPack 900 is the larger-capacity battery integrated into the Fjord Cargo, sized for the additional draw of hauling cargo loads. It uses the same standard 4A charger as the PowerPack 720, but because of its larger capacity a full charge from empty takes 5.5 hours rather than 4.5 hours. Its larger physical size and mounting hardware are specific to the Fjord Cargo's frame and are not interchangeable with the Aurora X3 or Vinter Pro. Standalone PowerPack 900 purchases are less common than PowerPack 720 purchases in absolute terms, simply reflecting the Fjord Cargo's smaller share of NordicBike's total bikes in the field, not any difference in the battery's own reliability.

## Compatibility

| Battery | Integrated into | Also sold standalone | Full charge time |
|---|---|---|---|
| PowerPack 720 | Aurora X3, Vinter Pro | Yes — 6,900 SEK | 4.5 hours |
| PowerPack 900 | Fjord Cargo | Yes — 8,900 SEK | 5.5 hours |

PowerPack batteries are not cross-compatible between the two models — a PowerPack 900 will not fit an Aurora X3 or Vinter Pro, and a PowerPack 720 will not fit a Fjord Cargo, due to differences in physical mounting and connector design between the two packs. Standalone accessory batteries purchased separately from a bike are not individually serialized in NordicBike's catalog; only bikes carry the [MODEL]-[YY][A|B]-[5 digits] serial format described on each bike's own product page (products/aurora-x3.md, products/fjord-cargo.md, products/vinter-pro.md). A standalone battery purchase is instead identified by its order ID and purchase date, which are sufficient to determine warranty status under policies/warranty.md Section 3.

## Related Policies

Spare/replacement batteries sold separately carry their own standalone warranty term — see policies/warranty.md Section 3. This differs from the whole-bike warranty term in Section 1. A support agent evaluating a standalone battery claim should confirm from the purchase record that the battery was in fact purchased on its own — not as part of a bike order — before applying Section 3; a battery listed as part of a bike's original purchase order is covered under that bike's Section 1 term instead, for the full 24 months from the bike's purchase date, not the shorter 12-month standalone term.

This is the single most common place in the entire NordicBike knowledge base where a support agent might default to the wrong warranty figure purely out of familiarity: the 24-month term appears so frequently across whole-bike cases that it is the number an agent is most likely to reach for reflexively, even when the case in front of them concerns a standalone battery governed by the shorter 12-month Section 3 term instead. Checking the purchase record for whether a battery was sold on its own or as part of a bike is a small, fast step that prevents this specific and easy-to-make mistake.
