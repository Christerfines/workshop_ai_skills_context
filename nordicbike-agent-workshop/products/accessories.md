# Accessories

## Overview

NordicBike AB sells a small catalog of riding accessories designed to complement the Aurora X3, Fjord Cargo, and Vinter Pro. These are optional add-on purchases — none is required for a bike to function, and none affects a bike's core warranty status. This document exists partly as a control: most customer support cases that reference warranty coverage are about the bike itself, not about an accessory, and a well-built support agent should recognize when the accessories catalog is irrelevant to a case rather than pulling it into context out of habit.

Each accessory in the catalog is designed to mount to the standard hardware points present on all three NordicBike bike models (subject to the Fjord Cargo's narrower rack-compatible subset noted in products/fjord-cargo.md), so a customer does not need to specify which bike they own when ordering an accessory — the same part number fits across the compatible models. Accessories can be purchased at the same time as a bike, added to an existing order before delivery, or purchased separately at any later point in ownership; the accessories catalog and its warranty terms are identical regardless of when the purchase happens relative to the bike itself.

None of the four items below are safety-critical in the way a bike's brakes or motor are — a customer riding without any of them is not riding an unsafe or non-compliant bike, with the partial exception of the frame lock, which is a security rather than a safety item, and the LED light set, which may be legally required equipment for riding after dark depending on local traffic regulations even though it is sold here as an optional accessory rather than bundled as standard equipment.

## Catalog

| Item | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Pannier bag | 1,200 SEK | Waterproof, 25L capacity, mounts to the rear rack |
| LED light set | 450 SEK | Front and rear lights, USB-rechargeable |
| Frame lock | 950 SEK | ART-approved integrated frame lock |
| Phone mount | 350 SEK | Weatherproof handlebar phone mount |

All four accessories are compatible with the Aurora X3 and Vinter Pro. The Fjord Cargo's compatible accessory set is narrower, given its different rack geometry; see products/fjord-cargo.md for which accessories apply to that model. The pannier bag is the highest-value accessory item in the catalog and the one most likely to appear in a returns-policy conversation, since it is a common add-on purchase made separately from a bike order.

The pannier bag's 25L capacity and waterproof construction make it the most commonly purchased accessory among Aurora X3 owners specifically, given the Aurora X3's positioning as a daily commuter and errand bike. The LED light set is USB-rechargeable rather than relying on disposable batteries, and is compatible with the same standard charging cable used for phones and other small USB devices, though it does not draw power from the bike's own main battery — it is charged independently. The frame lock is ART-approved, a recognized European security certification standard for bicycle locks, and mounts integrated into the frame rather than requiring a separate cable or chain to carry. The phone mount is weatherproof but is not itself a charging accessory — it holds a phone securely for navigation use during a ride but does not charge the phone's own battery.

## Accessories Warranty Note

Accessories carry a 6-month manufacturing-defect warranty only; wear and normal use are not covered. See policies/warranty.md.

This 6-month term applies uniformly across all four catalog items regardless of price or complexity — the pannier bag's zippers and waterproof seams, the LED light set's USB charging circuitry and light housing, the frame lock's locking mechanism, and the phone mount's clamping hardware are all covered on the same basis: a genuine manufacturing defect discovered within 6 months of purchase is covered, while wear from ordinary use (a zipper that eventually wears out after years of daily use, a lock mechanism that becomes stiffer with age and exposure) is not, in the same pattern as the wear-item logic described in policies/warranty.md Section 2 for bike components.

This 6-month accessory term is intentionally distinct from, and shorter than, every other warranty term described elsewhere in this knowledge base — shorter than the 24-month Standard Limited Warranty for whole bikes (policies/warranty.md Section 1), shorter than the 12-month standalone battery term (Section 3), and even shorter than the 6-month wear-item term for bike components, which it happens to match numerically but governs a conceptually different category of product. A support case that turns out to be about an accessory rather than the bike itself should be evaluated against this note, not against Section 1 or Section 2 of policies/warranty.md, even though an accessory is very often purchased and used alongside a bike that does carry those longer terms.
