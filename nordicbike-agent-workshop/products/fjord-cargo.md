# Fjord Cargo

## Overview

The Fjord Cargo is NordicBike AB's cargo e-bike, built for riders who need to move more than themselves — groceries, kids, tools, deliveries. It shares the Aurora X3's mid-drive motor architecture but is built around a reinforced frame and an extended rear rack designed to carry real cargo loads reliably, day after day, rather than as an occasional-use accessory bike. The longer wheelbase and lower standover height also make the Fjord Cargo easier to mount and dismount while it's loaded, which matters in practice for riders using it for daily errands rather than recreational rides.

The Fjord Cargo is the least common bike in the support case load of the four archetypes covered in this workshop, but it supplies an important nuance: NordicBike distinguishes between personal/private use of the standard Fjord Cargo and commercial or rental use, which is excluded from the standard warranty and requires a separate product tier entirely. This distinction reflects the real difference in stress a bike experiences under, for example, daily food-delivery use by multiple riders compared to a single household's grocery runs and school pickups — the standard Fjord Cargo's warranty assumptions are built around the latter.

NordicBike introduced the Fjord Cargo somewhat later than the Aurora X3, after observing that a meaningful share of Aurora X3 owners were asking about cargo capacity beyond what a pannier bag could reasonably provide — parents doing school pickups with more than one child, households replacing a second car for grocery runs, small tradespeople carrying tools between nearby jobs. Rather than stretching the Aurora X3's frame and rack rating to cover these cases, NordicBike built a dedicated model from the frame up, reinforced specifically for sustained cargo loads rather than occasional light use.

## Full Specifications

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| Motor | 250W mid-drive |
| Battery | PowerPack 900, integrated, 900Wh |
| Range | Up to 70 km |
| Top assisted speed | 25 km/h (EU pedelec limit) |
| Brakes | Hydraulic disc |
| Weight | 32 kg |
| Frame material | Aluminum 6061, reinforced |
| Cargo capacity | 100 kg (rear rack; optional front box sold separately, not included in this catalog) |
| Gearing | 8-speed derailleur |
| Display / connectivity | LCD display with companion app connectivity (Bluetooth) |
| Charger | Standard 4A charger, full charge in 4.5 hours |

The reinforced aluminum 6061 frame and longer wheelbase give the Fjord Cargo its 100 kg rated cargo capacity across the rear rack, whether carrying panniers, a rigid cargo box, or child seating hardware fitted by a service center. The hydraulic disc brakes are sized larger than the Aurora X3's to account for the additional stopping mass of a loaded cargo bike, and the reinforced frame's weld points around the rear rack mount are a common area of interest in structural warranty claims, given the repeated load-bearing stress that area experiences over years of daily cargo use. An optional front cargo box is available as an add-on but is out of scope for this catalog — no price is listed for it, and none should be assumed or invented.

At 32 kg unloaded, the Fjord Cargo is meaningfully heavier than the Aurora X3's 24 kg, a direct consequence of the reinforced frame, larger battery, and extended rack. The 250W mid-drive motor is the same nominal power rating as the Aurora X3's, but with the Fjord Cargo's gearing tuned slightly differently to account for the additional weight of both bike and typical cargo load, giving more usable low-end torque for pulling away from a stop with a loaded rack.

## Available Configurations

The Fjord Cargo is available in a single color only:

- **Color:** Slate Grey

Unlike the Aurora X3 and Vinter Pro, the Fjord Cargo is not offered in multiple frame sizes or color options at this time; all units share the same frame geometry, sized for a broad range of rider heights via an adjustable stem and saddle post. This single-configuration approach simplifies parts stocking for the service centers, since every Fjord Cargo in the field shares the same frame, rack, and mounting hardware, and it means a service center never needs to confirm frame size or color before ordering a replacement structural part — a meaningful simplification when a claim involves the rack or frame itself rather than a component shared across sizes.

## Battery & Range

The Fjord Cargo uses an integrated PowerPack 900 battery (900Wh) — a larger capacity pack than the Aurora X3's PowerPack 720, reflecting the additional draw of regularly hauling loaded cargo. Range under typical loaded conditions is up to 70 km on a full charge; range decreases with heavier cargo loads, headwinds, and cold weather, in the same general pattern as NordicBike's other e-bikes. A fully loaded Fjord Cargo at or near its 100 kg cargo capacity will see meaningfully lower range than an unloaded or lightly loaded ride, which is worth setting expectations around for customers planning longer errands or delivery routes.

The PowerPack 900 is not interchangeable with the PowerPack 720 used in the Aurora X3 and Vinter Pro — the two batteries use different physical mounting hardware and connector layouts specific to each bike's frame design, so a Fjord Cargo owner asking about a spare or replacement battery should always be pointed to the PowerPack 900 specifically, not the PowerPack 720. See products/powerpack-batteries.md for full standalone pricing and compatibility.

## Compatible Accessories

The Fjord Cargo is compatible with items in NordicBike's accessories catalog suited to cargo use (see products/accessories.md for full details and pricing), including the frame lock and LED light set. The optional front cargo box referenced under Full Specifications above is not part of the fixed accessories catalog and is out of scope for this document. The pannier bag and phone mount from the standard accessories catalog are also physically compatible with the Fjord Cargo's rack and handlebar, though most Fjord Cargo owners purchasing significant additional cargo capacity look toward the (out-of-scope) front box rather than a pannier bag alone, given the rack's already substantial capacity on its own.

## Business Edition Note

The standard Fjord Cargo warranty (see policies/warranty.md) excludes commercial/rental use. A separate Fjord Cargo Business Edition (not covered by this spec) permits commercial use under warranty.

A customer describing regular use of their Fjord Cargo for a delivery service, a rental fleet, or any other commercial purpose should be asked whether they purchased the standard Fjord Cargo or the Business Edition, since this document and the standard warranty terms in policies/warranty.md apply only to the standard edition. The Business Edition is a distinct product line with its own terms not covered anywhere in this knowledge base; a support agent encountering a genuine Business Edition case should treat it as outside the scope of the documentation available and escalate for guidance rather than applying the standard Fjord Cargo's terms by assumption.
